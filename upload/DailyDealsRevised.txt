PROJECT BRIEF: "SNAPJE" - HYPER-LOCAL FOOD FLASH DEAL APP (FOUNDER MVP → SCALE ROADMAP)
OBJECTIVE: Build a hyper-local, time-sensitive food flash deal ecosystem that connects hungry consumers (especially office workers and urban professionals) with nearby food vendors who need to liquidate unsold inventory or pre-sell upcoming meal batches.
The business model is designed around predictable recurring revenue (MRR), not transaction commissions.
PRIMARY REVENUE STREAMS:
	•	Vendor SaaS Subscription:
	◦	Basic Tier: RM99/month.
	◦	Premium Tier: RM199/month.
	•	Consumer Premium Membership:
	◦	"First Dibs Pass" subscription providing early access to high-demand flash deals.
	•	Future B2B Corporate Subscription:
	◦	Office Pods and workplace group ordering solutions.
CORE BUSINESS THESIS: A vendor losing 20 unsold meals experiences a 100% loss.
Selling those meals at a 40–60% discount still creates incremental revenue, reduces food waste, and introduces the vendor to new customers.
SNAPJE is not competing directly as another food delivery marketplace. The long-term strategic moat is becoming the workplace lunch discovery and coordination network.
PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY:
Phase 0 - Concierge Validation (No-Code):
	•	Validate market demand before large engineering investment.
	•	Recruit 10–20 food vendors.
	•	Recruit 50–100 office workers.
	•	Test:
	◦	What discount percentages convert best.
	◦	Which meal categories have highest demand.
	◦	Which time windows produce the highest engagement.
	◦	Whether vendors are willing to pay a monthly subscription.
Phase 1 - Core Revenue MVP: Build only the minimum system required to validate the SaaS business.
User:
	•	Discover nearby deals.
	•	View food details.
	•	Claim deals.
	•	Receive QR pickup code.
Vendor:
	•	Complete onboarding.
	•	Submit business information.
	•	Subscribe to a vendor plan.
	•	Create flash deals.
	•	Manage available stock.
	•	Verify customer pickup via QR scanning.
Admin:
	•	Review vendor applications.
	•	Perform KYC verification.
	•	Approve or suspend vendors.
	•	Manage subscriptions.
Phase 2 - Growth Features:
	•	First Dibs Pass.
	•	Personalized recommendations.
	•	Vendor analytics dashboard.
	•	Basic marketing tools.
	•	Improved geolocation ranking.
Phase 3 - Network Effects & Platform Expansion:
	•	Office Pods.
	•	Corporate subscriptions.
	•	Siren Push hyper-local marketing.
	•	Auto-Drop liquidation engine.
	•	Rider ecosystem (only if delivery economics justify it).
ONE APP, ONE ACCOUNT, MULTIPLE ROLES: The system uses dynamic Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
A single user identity can possess multiple roles:
	•	Customer.
	•	Vendor.
	•	Office Pod Leader.
	•	Rider (future).
	•	Administrator.
The interface dynamically transforms based on the currently selected role.

========================================================================= 5. CORE ENGINE LOGIC (THE SCALE FEATURES)

	1	LIVE INVENTORY ENGINE

Technology:
	•	PostgreSQL transactional inventory.
	•	Redis temporary reservation storage.
	•	Socket.io real-time broadcasting.
Flow:
User claims meal ↓ Create reservation ↓ Lock quantity temporarily ↓ Broadcast updated availability ↓ User completes claim ↓ Generate QR order
If reservation expires:
Reservation timeout reached ↓ Release quantity ↓ Return stock to available inventory ↓ Broadcast new inventory count
Benefits:
	•	Prevents overselling.
	•	Maintains fairness.
	•	Preserves trust.

	2	FIRST DIBS EARLY ACCESS ENGINE (PHASE 2)

NO CRON JOBS.
Every deal stores two visibility timestamps:
Example:
early_access_at: 11:45 AM
public_access_at: 12:00 PM
Access Logic:
Premium User:
Current time >= early_access_at
Result: Deal visible.
Free User:
Current time >= public_access_at
Result: Deal visible.
UI Indicators:
Before public release: "🔓 First Dibs Exclusive"
After public release: Standard deal display.

	3	SIREN PUSH HYPER-LOCAL MARKETING (PHASE 3)

Purpose: Allow vendors to instantly liquidate urgent inventory.
Vendor Action:
Press: "Siren Push"
Targeting Logic:
Find users who satisfy:
Distance: Highest priority: 0–1 km.
Secondary: 1–3 km.
Extended: 3–5 km.
Only expand further when inventory remains high.
Additional Signals:
	•	Previous purchase categories.
	•	Favorite vendors.
