     1→PROJECT BRIEF: "FLASHBITE" - HYPER-LOCAL FOOD FLASH DEAL APP (FOUNDER MVP → SCALE ROADMAP)
     2→OBJECTIVE: Build a hyper-local, time-sensitive food flash deal ecosystem that connects hungry consumers (especially office workers and urban professionals) with nearby food vendors who need to liquidate unsold inventory or pre-sell upcoming meal batches.
     3→The business model is designed around predictable recurring revenue (MRR), not transaction commissions.
     4→PRIMARY REVENUE STREAMS:
     5→        •       Vendor SaaS Subscription:
     6→        ◦       Basic Tier: RM99/month.
     7→        ◦       Premium Tier: RM199/month.
     8→        •       Consumer Premium Membership:
     9→        ◦       "First Dibs Pass" subscription providing early access to high-demand flash deals.
    10→        •       Future B2B Corporate Subscription:
    11→        ◦       Office Pods and workplace group ordering solutions.
    12→CORE BUSINESS THESIS: A vendor losing 20 unsold meals experiences a 100% loss.
    13→Selling those meals at a 40–60% discount still creates incremental revenue, reduces food waste, and introduces the vendor to new customers.
    14→FLASHBITE is not competing directly as another food delivery marketplace. The long-term strategic moat is becoming the workplace lunch discovery and coordination network.
    15→PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY:
    16→Phase 0 - Concierge Validation (No-Code):
    17→        •       Validate market demand before large engineering investment.
    18→        •       Recruit 10–20 food vendors.
    19→        •       Recruit 50–100 office workers.
    20→        •       Test:
    21→        ◦       What discount percentages convert best.
    22→        ◦       Which meal categories have highest demand.
    23→        ◦       Which time windows produce the highest engagement.
    24→        ◦       Whether vendors are willing to pay a monthly subscription.
    25→Phase 1 - Core Revenue MVP: Build only the minimum system required to validate the SaaS business.
    26→User:

    43→Phase 2 - Growth Features:

   767→        1       LIVE INVENTORY ENGINE
   768→
   769→Technology:
   770→        •       PostgreSQL transactional inventory.
   771→        •       Redis temporary reservation storage.
   772→        •       Socket.io real-time broadcasting.
   773→Flow:
   774→User claims meal ↓ Create reservation ↓ Lock quantity temporarily ↓ Broadcast updated availability ↓ User completes claim ↓ Generate QR order
   775→If reservation expires:
   776→Reservation timeout reached ↓ Release quantity ↓ Return stock to available inventory ↓ Broadcast new inventory count
   777→Benefits:
   778→        •       Prevents overselling.
   779→        •       Maintains fairness.
   780→        •       Preserves trust.

   789→Access Logic:
   790→Premium User:
   791→Current time >= early_access_at
   792→Result: Deal visible.
   793→Free User:
   794→Current time >= public_access_at
   795→Result: Deal visible.
